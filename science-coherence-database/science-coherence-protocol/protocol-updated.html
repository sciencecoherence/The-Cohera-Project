5. Cardiac Electrophysiology & Pharmacokinetic Dynamics

The hERG (IKr) Blockade & QTc Dynamics

Ibogaine and active metabolite noribogaine act as potent blockers of the human ether-à-go-go-related gene (hERG / IKr) voltage-gated potassium channel in cardiac myocytes, delaying Phase 3 repolarization and prolonging the QTc interval.

Potassium Loading Shield: High-normal serum potassium (K+ > 4.5\text{ mEq/L}) via fresh coconut water and raw fish enhances outward repolarizing currents and physically impedes noribogaine binding within the hERG channel pore.

Tauromag: Myocardial Membrane Shield

Magnesium Acetyl-taurate / Taurate provides direct myocardial and central protection when administered in the evening:

  • Magnesium (Mg2+): Acts as a physiological calcium antagonist, stabilizing resting potential and blocking Early Afterdepolarizations (EADs) to prevent Torsades de Pointes.
  • Taurine: Regulates the Na+\text{-}Ca2+ exchanger (NCX), preventing intracellular calcium overload and safeguarding myocardial potassium retention.
