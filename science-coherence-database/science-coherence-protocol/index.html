3.1 Active TET1–3 Demethylation & TDG Base Excision

Enzymatic Erasure

Active reversal of hypermethylation drift is driven by Ten-Eleven Translocation (TET1, TET2, TET3) dioxygenases[cite: 1]. Utilizing intracellular \(\alpha\)-ketoglutarate (\(\alpha\)-KG), \(O_2\), and \(Fe^{2+}\) cofactors, TET enzymes iteratively oxidize 5-methylcytosine (\(5\text{mC}\))[cite: 1]:

$$5\text{mC} \xrightarrow{\text{TET}} 5\text{hmC} \xrightarrow{\text{TET}} 5\text{fC} \xrightarrow{\text{TET}} 5\text{caC}$$

Thymine DNA Glycosylase (TDG) cleaves the \(N\)-glycosidic bond of \(5\text{fC}\) and \(5\text{caC}\), generating an apurinic/apyrimidinic (AP) abasic site[cite: 1]. APE1 / Ref-1 then hydrolyzes the phosphodiester backbone \(5'\) to the lesion, creating a single-strand break with a \(3'\text{-OH}\) primer terminus and a blocking \(5'\text{-dRP}\) flap[cite: 1].

💡 Plain English (ELI5): "The Rust Eraser"

Think of DNA as a master cookbook. As cells get older, sticky chemical rust spots (methyl marks) gum up the recipe pages so the cell can't read them. TET enzymes act like a rust converter, turning the hard rust into brittle chalk. Then, TDG acts like a hole punch, popping the rusted letter clean out of the page so it's ready for a brand-new letter.

3.2 Physical Gap Resynthesis via Polymerase-β

BER Gap-Filling

DNA Polymerase β (\(\text{Pol }\beta\)) executes Base Excision Repair (BER) gap filling via a bifunctional, dual-domain catalytic architecture[cite: 1]:

  • \(8\text{ kDa}\) Amino Lyase: Excises the blocking \(5'\text{-dRP}\) residue via β-elimination to expose a clean \(5'\text{-phosphate}\).
  • \(31\text{ kDa}\) Polymerase Domain: Coordinates dual \(\text{Mg}^{2+}\) ions to insert an unmodified deoxycytidine monophosphate (dCTP) opposite the template guanine[cite: 1]:
$$\text{Gap}_{(3'\text{-OH} / 5'\text{-P})} + \text{dCTP} \xrightarrow{\text{Pol }\beta, \text{Mg}^{2+}} \text{DNA}_{\text{inserted}} + \text{PP}_i$$

The DNA Ligase III / XRCC1 complex seals the phosphodiester backbone, permanently restoring an unmethylated, youthful cytosine base[cite: 1].

💡 Plain English (ELI5): "The Precision Typewriter"

After the rusted letter is popped out, the cell needs to replace it. \(\text{Pol }\beta\) is a tiny two-handed typewriter: its left hand snips away the torn paper edge, and its right hand stamps in a fresh, unblemished letter 'C' (dCTP). Molecular glue (DNA Ligase) then seals the paper smooth.

4.4 Fascial Mechanics & Electrokinetic Streaming Potentials (\(V_{str}\))

Because collagen fibrils and proteoglycans possess a fixed negative charge density (FCD), mechanical shearing and stretching displace mobile hydronium counter-ions (\(H_3O^+\)) through the interstitial ground substance, generating an endogenous electric field governed by the Helmholtz-Smoluchowski equation[cite: 1]:
