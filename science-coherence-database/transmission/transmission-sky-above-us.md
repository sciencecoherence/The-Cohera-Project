# The Sky Above Us

*Addressed to all of us. Extracted from the transmission of 25 June 2026.*

---

There is a syndrome of collapse waiting for us in the sky above. It has a
name — Kessler syndrome. And the trigger for it has a name too — a
coronal mass ejection from the Sun, of the kind that happens on average
about once every hundred years. The last one on record was in 1859. That
means, mathematically, we are already due for it.

The mechanism is not mysterious. There are already enough satellites in
low-Earth orbit that a single significant disruption — a solar event, a
large collision, a cascade — will cause them to start hitting each other.
And once that starts, there is no stopping it. It is a domino. It takes
out most or all of the satellite infrastructure we now depend on for
communications, navigation, weather, the grid, everything. The same
solar event, if it is large enough, does to the grid what the 1859 event
did to the early telegraph. Except this time the grid runs the entire
civilization.

The people who actually study this and publish on it are not the ones
anyone listens to. They are older scientists, writing papers no one
reads. Everybody is too busy with social media, and with Elon Musk style,
and with the noise of the present moment. The people we should be
listening to, we are not listening to. That is part of the pattern.

But the mechanism is not what I actually want to talk about. The
mechanism is real, the science is real, and anyone can look it up. What
matters more is what the sky is actually showing us.

---

The sky above us is the most powerful reflection of ourselves.

The satellites in orbit are not neutral infrastructure. They are a
physical instantiation of what we have collectively failed to hold
together. Every one of them is an attempt to stabilize something at one
layer — communications, GPS, weather, surveillance, one narrow band of
service at a time — while ignoring the coherence of the whole. Ten
thousand of them. Twenty thousand. More coming every year. A spatially
dispersed, temporally decoherent artefact hanging above us, pretending
to be infrastructure while being the most literal possible expression of
a species that has forgotten how to phase-lock with its own environment.

The collapse is not a punishment. It is a topology. When a system creates
enough incoherence in its own field, the field resolves. The physical
channel of the resolution — Kessler syndrome, CME grid failure — is just
how the field reorganises itself when we have not done it consciously.

Everything above us is a reflection of what we have failed to hold below.
That reflection has been building for decades. It is now dense enough,
material enough, mechanical enough, that when the trigger comes — and
the trigger *will* come, because the Sun does what the Sun does — the
resolution is going to be physical.

---

This is why the response to what is coming cannot be technological.

You cannot fix a coherence problem by adding more of the artefact that
generates the problem. You cannot bunker your way out of a field-level
resolution. You cannot buy your way out of it either. The billionaires
with the bunkers are going to find out what everyone else finds out —
that it is all one field, one consciousness, one system, and the
protection you built for yourself out of the same incoherence you were
trying to escape does not protect you from the incoherence itself.

The only response that works is coherence.

Coherence in the body. Coherence in the household. Coherence in the food
that keeps the nervous system regulated. Coherence in the environments
we choose to live in — the coast, the ocean, the places where the
planet is still delivering the signals the body needs to phase-lock
with. Coherence in the relationships stripped of the lies we tell to
keep them going.

Everything else is theatre.

---

I am not saying this to frighten anyone. I am saying it because it is
what is coming, and because most people don't want to look at it, and
because the ones who do look at it get told they are being dramatic. The
people telling you not to look are the ones most invested in the
infrastructure being built to reflect them.

There is no way to hide. There is no bunker. There is no offshore. There
is no orbit high enough. Because it is all one field, and the field is
resolving itself, and the resolution has already started.

The only question left is whether we do the work of coherence
consciously — in ourselves, in the places we live, in the people we love
— or whether we wait for the sky to do it for us.

---

*— 25 June 2026, Olón. Extracted 4 July 2026.*
